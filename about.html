OUR STORY

More Than Coffee. It's Hospitality.

BeiRoot's Café was created with a simple idea: to create a warm and welcoming place where people can enjoy great coffee, delicious food and genuine Lebanese hospitality.

Our café brings together the comfort of a neighborhood café with flavors and traditions inspired by Lebanon. From a traditional Lebanese coffee to specialty drinks and desserts, every visit is meant to feel welcoming, relaxed and memorable.
